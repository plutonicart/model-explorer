Protocol and interpretation

100 h equilibration without drug. Each dose starts independently from its matched baseline. Readout after 24 h for PSN1 and 48 h for MCF7. These are the application conventions; their correspondence to the figure-generation protocol is unconfirmed.

PSN1: RAS-GTP 300 nM. MCF7: RAS-GTP 35 nM. KSR1: 0, 15, 30, 50 or 100 nM. RAFi parameters: Supplementary Table S3. Cobimetinib: author-export fM=1 by default; Table S3 fM=0.1 is retained as a separate comparison. ppERK is the native model observable, including bound double-phosphorylated ERK. Each curve is normalized to its own matched no-drug control. X is free inhibitor / model Kd; absolute drug concentration is not calibrated.

Numerical checks cover baseline equilibrium, conservation of protein totals, valid molecular amounts and tighter solver tolerances. Numerical agreement with the author equations and agreement with the published raster figure are distinct checks.

Differences in the table are percentage points of no-drug control, evaluated at the calculated doses that lie within the extracted trace. Raster error and line overlap limit their precision.

Source: Imoto et al., Dual RAF inhibition outperforms RAF-MEK combinations for suppressing ERK signaling in KRAS mutant cells. Author model, commit 194915876d7b6d907e8f7a0fd5e78b78ce75c1b7.
